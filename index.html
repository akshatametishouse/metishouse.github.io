Strategy for consumer and culture-driven organizations

Metis House is the independent advisory practice of Akshata Prabhu (former Bain & Company). I advise leadership teams in consumer and culture-driven sectors on three questions: what to offer, how to compete, and where to invest.

Metis House has partnered with 15+ clients across the US, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Central Asia, and India, in sectors spanning cultural heritage, higher education, beauty, food, hospitality, entertainment, and e-commerce. I work directly with C-suite and C-1 leaders, often serving as their interim strategy lead. Engagements are lean by design: I lead each one personally and bring in a trusted network of experts, analysts, and vendors when a mandate calls for it.

Metis House is most suited for:

  • Investors who need sharp consumer and sector judgment for theses, commercial due diligence, or portfolio value creation, without the overhead of a full consulting team.
  • Corporate leaders looking to sharpen a value proposition, revitalize a brand, enter a new market, or find their path to market.
  • Public sector organizations building institutions, assets, and experiences around public value and national or cultural identity.
  • Consulting firms in need of senior consumer or regional expertise to lead or strengthen a specific engagement.

Experience

Expertise

Metis House’s expertise spans corporate and business unit strategy, including portfolio, positioning, and operating model design; consumer insight and trends, brand strategy, and proposition design; commercial due diligence, sector theses, and value creation for investors; and new ventures and 0-to-1 initiative design, including market entry and consumer adoption.

This draws from my time at Bain & Company, where my stint spanned two practices: Private Equity & Venture Capital: this spanned 25+ commercial due diligences for global investors across the US, EMEA, and APAC, and 10+ strategic planning and growth engagements for Fortune 500 leaders in beauty, apparel, food and beverages, and e-commerce.

Working Together

No two mandates are alike, so no two engagements are either. Depending on the need, I work directly with leadership, embed into an existing team, or lead a lean Metis House team drawn from a select bench of former MBB colleagues, sector specialists, and analysts. I also partner with established consulting firms in a freelance leadership role, bringing regional and sectoral expertise to their mandates.

Metis House takes on three to four engagements a year, so every client has senior attention from start to finish, with every engagement led by me personally.

Engagements begin with an exploratory conversation, followed by a proposal clarifying the approach, work plan, deliverables, and fees. Confidentiality terms are agreed in writing at the outset.

Based in Dubai, Metis House works with clients across the Middle East, Central and South Asia, the UK, and the US.
