Metis House

Strategy for consumer and culture-driven organizations

Metis House is the strategy firm founded by Akshata Prabhu (ex-Bain & Company), advising leadership teams in consumer and culture-driven sectors on their biggest strategic choices, from what to offer to how to compete and where to invest.

To date, the firm has partnered with over fifteen organizations across the Middle East, Europe, North America, and Central and South Asia, in sectors that shape how people live and spend their time: across cultural heritage, higher education, beauty, personal care, food and beverages, hospitality, entertainment, quick commerce and more.

Metis House is most suited for:

  • Investors who need sharp consumer and sector judgment for theses, commercial due diligence, or portfolio value creation.
  • Corporate leaders looking to sharpen a value proposition, revitalize a brand, enter a new market, or find their path to market.
  • Public sector organizations building institutions, assets, and experiences around public value and national or cultural identity.
  • Consulting firms in need of senior consumer or regional expertise to lead or strengthen a specific engagement.
