Working Together

Metis House takes on a small number of mandates each year, each led personally by me. Depending on the work, that means advising leadership directly, embedding within the client’s team, or assembling a lean team from a close bench of former MBB colleagues and sector specialists. I also collaborate selectively with established consulting firms where my regional and sector depth adds to their work.

Based in Dubai, Metis House works with clients across the Middle East, Central and South Asia, the UK, and the US.
