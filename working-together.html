Working Together

Based in Dubai, Metis House works with clients across the Middle East, Central and South Asia, the UK, and the US, and takes on a small number of mandates each year, each led personally by Akshata Prabhu.

Depending on the work, that means advising leadership directly, embedding within the client’s team, or assembling a lean team from a close bench of former MBB colleagues and sector specialists. Metis House also collaborates selectively with established consulting firms where its regional and sector depth adds to their work.

Most engagements begin through referral. For new enquiries, write to mail@metis.house or reach out on LinkedIn.
